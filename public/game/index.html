BOGAN BAY / VICTORIA

Pick your grip.

Four spring-damped wheels on a rigid-body chassis. The ute has planted 4x4 grip and a diesel soundtrack. The V8 sedan keeps loose rear tyres for power slides. Brake before a bend and countersteer the Holden. Hold B or W + S for deliberate line-lock burnouts; release to bank a combo.

Left click throws punches, or draws down with the two simple guns — press 1 for the pistol and 2 for the rifle, left click to fire, press the number again to holster. Locals fight back, lose health and get knocked down; your health returns when things settle. Drag to face your next swing. On phones, tap PUNCH. Most of the old screen clutter is gone: press Q for the phone (jobs, map, travel, controls) and M for the map.

At home you can sit on the couch or a stool (E), rip the bong for an hour of serenity or skull a beer from the fridge — too many and you're maggot, wobbling all over the road and barred from driving until it wears off. Swim out past the shallows and hold SPACE to dive under and check out the coral, schools and the dolphin pod; release to float back up. Traffic now brakes for pedestrians and your car. E plays a short door-opening and car-entry sequence.
